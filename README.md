## Project Overview

This project analyzes B2B sales data across major Indian markets from 2017 to 2020 covering ₹985M in revenue with ₹25M total profit. The dashboard tracks revenue distribution, sales quantity, and profit performance across 13 Indian cities, identifies top customers and products, and surfaces market-level profitability patterns — including negative profit markets requiring strategic attention.

## Key Findings

**1. Delhi NCR drives 52.8% of total revenue — extreme market concentration**
Over half of all revenue comes from a single market. This concentration creates significant business risk — any Delhi NCR disruption would impact overall performance severely.

**2. Bengaluru is losing money at -20.8% profit margin**
Bengaluru is the most critical red flag in this dataset. Despite generating some revenue, the market is deeply unprofitable — requiring immediate investigation of pricing, cost structure, or customer mix.

**3. Electricalsara Stores alone contributes ₹413.3M — 42% of total revenue**
Single customer concentration this high (one customer = 42% of revenue) is a major business risk. Loss of this one account would be catastrophic.

**4. Overall profit % is only 3% despite ₹985M revenue**
Extremely thin margins suggest either highly competitive pricing, high cost of goods, or unprofitable markets dragging down the average — Bengaluru's -20.8% being the primary culprit.

**5. Revenue trend shows consistent decline from ₹43M peak (2018) to ₹15M by 2020**
The downward trend across 3 years signals a structural business problem — not a temporary dip — requiring strategic intervention in pricing, market mix, or product portfolio.

**6. Surat delivers the highest profit % (4.9%) but minimal revenue contribution**
Surat is the most profitable market per rupee of revenue but remains a very small market. Expanding Surat operations could improve overall margin quality significantly.
